# CM Storage Presets
**CM Storage** lets you create inventory and equipment presets per room in CM Chambers.

The plugin can rearrange your private storage iinto a zigzag layout or highlight items that are missing from your setup. You could also just mark individual items for deposit or withdraw in specific rooms.

# Configuration

## General Settings

- **Storage Options**: Adds the 'Save Setup' and 'Clear Setup' to every storage chest in the raid.

## Panel Settings
- **Auto Load Rooms**: Automatically displays the correct side-panel when entering rooms.

## Highlight Settings
- **Highlight Mismatched Items**: Places a box around items that do not match your setup.
- **Highlight Equipment**: Highlights equipment from your preset that you haven't equipped.
- **Highlight Inventory**: Highlights items from your preset that are in your private storage.
- **Highlight Withdraw / Deposit**: Highlights items that you have marked in your setup.
- **Ghost Outlines**: Toggles outlines on ghost items in your private storage.

## ZigZag Settings
- **Enable ZigZag**: Arranges your items in a zigzag pattern in private storage.
- **ZigZag Equipment**: Includes your equipment in the zigzag layout.
- **ZigZag Inventory**: Includes your items in the zigzag layout.
- **Compact ZigZag**: Display nine items per line rather than eight.
- **Standardise Potions**: Makes potions use alternatives if possible.
